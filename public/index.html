Reconstruction method, conventions and limitations ＋

2D FFT → filtering disks → demodulation of both orders → arg(H · conj(Href)) → calibrated gradients → rotation → spectral least-squares integration. Intensity comes from the zero order; with a reference, T/Tref is displayed.

The wavefront is expressed as OPD (nm), with piston and mean tilt removed. Integration uses periodic boundary conditions: objects cut off at the edges may cause artifacts. No gradient unwrapping or vortex handling is applied. Dark regions may make phase estimates unreliable.

Images are center-cropped to power-of-two dimensions, up to 1024 × 1024, without resampling. Both files must have the same original dimensions. Magnification sets the object-space scale, not the OPD amplitude. For measurements, use linear camera TIFF data and an experimental calibration of α.

Scientific basis: G. Baffou, J. Phys. D: Appl. Phys. 54 (2021) 294002, §5. The supplied MATLAB screenshot uses α = 0.52 without specifying its unit; its convention must be confirmed before quantitative use.
